# gloomy

gloomy turns the whole screen into a single, very dim color. It is meant for finding your way in
the dark without disturbing your night vision or raising your vigilance, e.g., checking on a
sleeping child without blinding yourself or anyone else, or getting a glass of water at night.

## Controls

- Swipe up or down, or use the volume keys, to make the light brighter or dimmer.
- Swipe left or right to move continuously through the spectrum, from red
  through orange, yellow, green, cyan and blue to violet, and finally white.

## Features

- Red by default, which preserves night vision best.
- Goes dimmer than the phone's lowest brightness setting by also dimming the
  color itself.
- Keeps the screen on and hides the status and navigation bars while open.
- Remembers the last color and brightness.
- Changes the screen brightness only while gloomy is open.
- No permissions, no network access, no ads, no tracking.
