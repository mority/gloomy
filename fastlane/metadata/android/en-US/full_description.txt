gloomy turns the whole screen into a single, very dim color. It is meant for finding your way in
the dark without disturbing your night vision or raising your vigilance, e.g., checking on a
sleeping child without blinding yourself or anyone else, or getting a glass of water at night.

<b>Controls</b>
<ul>
<li>Swipe up or down, or use the volume keys, to make the light brighter or dimmer.</li>
<li>Swipe left or right to move continuously from red through amber and
yellow to white.</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Red by default, which preserves night vision best.</li>
<li>Goes dimmer than the phone's lowest brightness setting by also dimming the
color itself.</li>
<li>Keeps the screen on and hides the status and navigation bars while open.</li>
<li>Remembers the last color and brightness.</li>
<li>Changes the screen brightness only while gloomy is open.</li>
<li>No permissions, no network access, no ads, no tracking.</li>
</ul>
